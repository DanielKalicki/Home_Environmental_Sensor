Only pressure, temperature and humidity are measured. Everything else in this section is derived by Bosch's BSEC library from the resistance of the sensor's heated film, which falls as volatile compounds in the air increase but also moves with humidity and drifts as the film ages. BSEC corrects for that against a baseline it learns from the last few days, so the indoor air quality index, the CO₂ equivalent and the VOC figure are estimates relative to the air this sensor usually sits in, not absolute concentrations. The CO₂ equivalent in particular is inferred from the gas signal; the SCD41 above measures CO₂ directly and is the figure to trust. Temperature and humidity are measured independently of the SCD41, so the two sections will not agree exactly.
